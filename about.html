About Me

Hello! I'm a student, and thinker based in Bengaluru. My primary interests lie at the intersection of technology, design, and storytelling. I believe that the best work comes from a place of curiosity and a desire to build things that are both useful and beautiful.

When I'm not coding or studying, you can find me exploring local coffee shops, reading science fiction, or trying to learn a new song on the guitar.

My Philosophy

I strive to approach everything with a "beginner's mind." This means being open to new ideas, not being afraid to ask questions, and understanding that there's always more to learn.
